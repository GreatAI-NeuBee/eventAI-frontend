PNG to SVG Converter

Convert your Canva PNG export to SVG with circles and rectangles. Perfect for simple venue layouts.

How to use:

1. Upload your PNG from Canva
2. Auto-detection will find circles and rectangles automatically
3. Move and edit the detected shapes as needed
4. Add more shapes manually if required
5. Download the final SVG and continue to the Attribute Tagger

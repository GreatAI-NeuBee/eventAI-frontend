Design Your Venue Layout in Canva

Draw zones (polygons), exits (circles), and toilets (circles) visually. Export SVG from Canva, then tag it with metadata so our importer can read it.

Step 1 — Open Canva

Start a design, keep shapes simple (polygons for zones, circles for exits & toilets), avoid flatten/masks.

Open Canva Download SVG Template (optional)

Tip: Upload the SVG template into Canva and just reposition/resize shapes.

Step 2 — Export from Canva

Choose Download → SVG. Your file should include a viewBox on the root <svg>.

Step 3 — Add required metadata

Canva SVGs are visual-only. Add class and data-* attributes using this tool:

Open Attribute Tagger

This injects class="zone|exit|toilet" and attributes like data-id, data-name, data-layer so your importer can parse it.

Importer Requirements (summary)

Zones (polygons)

<polygon class="zone" data-id="z-101" data-name="North A" data-layer="1" points="x,y ..." />

Exits / Toilets (circles)

<circle class="exit" data-id="exit-1" data-name="Gate 1" cx="…" cy="…" r="…" />
<circle class="toilet" data-id="wc-1" data-label="WC 1" cx="…" cy="…" r="…" />

Coordinates can be any reasonable numbers; the importer normalizes to 100×62.5 space.
